About Karma Internationals
Makeup Masterclass

Karma Internationals Makeup Masterclass is happening on August 4th-5th, 2025, at the Biswa Bangla Exhibition Centre in Newtown, Kolkata. This event is described as "Asia's Biggest Cosmetic & Salon Expo" and will feature makeup artists like Suman Adhikary, who will be doing a live demonstration on August 4th, and Sanjoy Debnath. The event is aimed at beauty enthusiasts, professionals, and innovators, offering opportunities to discover, learn, and get inspired.
