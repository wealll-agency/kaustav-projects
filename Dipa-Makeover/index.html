About Me

Dipas Makeover And Academy – The Best Bridal Makeup Artist & Makeup Academy in Kolkata

When it comes to bridal beauty, perfection is non-negotiable. Dipas Makeover And Academy has set the gold standard in Kolkata with over 10 years of experience in the makeup industry. Renowned as the Best Bridal Makeup Artist in Kolkata, Dipa has transformed the looks of over 1000 brides and women, ensuring that each one radiates confidence and elegance on their special day.

Dipa’s approach to bridal makeup is a seamless blend of tradition and modernity. She understands that every bride is unique, and her makeup should reflect her personality while enhancing her natural beauty. Whether it's a classic Bengali bridal look with bold red lips and intricate eye makeup or a contemporary, minimalistic approach, Dipa’s artistry ensures a flawless finish that lasts throughout the wedding festivities.

Her attention to detail, skin-friendly techniques, and use of high-quality, professional-grade products make her a preferred choice for brides in Kolkata and beyond. She has traveled across states, beautifying brides for their big day, and her expertise is backed by rave reviews from happy clients who praise her ability to create stunning yet comfortable looks.
